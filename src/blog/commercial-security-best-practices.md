Commercial security requires a proactive architecture built for 24/7 reliability. When safeguarding retail inventory, office facilities, or industrial warehouses across Belleville and the Quinte region, enterprise-grade video surveillance and smart access control are essential.

## Key Planning Considerations

1. **Full Color Night Vision**: Hikvision ColorVu sensors capture vivid color video telemetry even in complete pitch darkness without relying on intrusive white spotlights.
2. **Encrypted Local NVR Storage**: Eliminate recurring monthly cloud subscription fees by storing encrypted high definition footage on site with redundant hard drives.
3. **Smart Door Access Control**: Grant digital keycard or mobile app credentials to staff with customizable scheduled time windows and audit logging.
