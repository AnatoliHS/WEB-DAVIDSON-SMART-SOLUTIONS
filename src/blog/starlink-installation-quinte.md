Living in Prince Edward County or rural Belleville offers beautiful scenery, but high-speed internet can be a challenge. SpaceX's **Starlink** satellite service provides gigabit speeds, but improper dish mounting and cable routing can lead to signal dropouts during high winds or snow.

## Why Professional Mounting Matters

- **Heavy Duty Roof & Chimney Mounts**: Engineered to withstand Canadian winter ice and high wind gusts.
- **Concealed Weather-Proof Cable Entry**: Safe low-voltage wall penetrations to prevent water leaks.
- **Enterprise Wi-Fi Integration**: Connecting your Starlink dish directly to an Araknis mesh network so gigabit speeds reach every room.
