All Connected. Not Siloed.

Imagine sitting in bed reading. When you're ready to sleep, you simply tap "Bedtime" on your phone, voice assistant, universal remote, or wall keypad.

Within seconds, motorized shades lower, ambient lights fade to zero, deadbolts lock, security arms, and climate control drops to your preferred nighttime temperature — all without getting out of bed. All smart technology connected in one elegant interface rather than fragmented across dozens of separate apps.

  • Single App & Touchpanel Control (Control4 & Lutron)
  • One Touch Scene Automations ("Bedtime", "Away", "Entertainment")
  • Seamless Voice, Keypad, Remote & Smartphone Access

Unlock the Potential of Smart Homes

A professionally installed smart home system automatically adapts brightness and color temperature throughout the day and per season. Quick-access climate controls keep every zone in your home optimal based on occupancy and sun exposure.
