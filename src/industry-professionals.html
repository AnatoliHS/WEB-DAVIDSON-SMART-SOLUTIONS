Technology Disappears, Your Design Shines

Bulky switches, visible wires, awkwardly placed sensors, or massive black screens can clash with your vision and ruin carefully planned sightlines and focal points.

Davidson Smart Solutions sets you apart from other interior designers by carefully incorporating technology into architecture and decor so it supports the space:

  • Automated architectural lighting & circadian daylighting
  • Custom motorized shades & drapery matching textiles
  • Hidden, crystal-clear architectural in ceiling audio
  • Clean, refined home theatres & artwork TV concealments
  • Discreet home security, glass break & environmental sensors
