A

Editorial Spotlight

One review at a time, set large in a refined serif like a magazine pull-quote. The reviewer names form a selector with a progress line that auto-advances. It's quiet, confident and very high-end.

Best for
A small set of strong reviews that each deserve the stage
Strength
The most premium feel. Every word gets read
Trade-off
Only one review is visible at a time

B

Wall of Praise

Three columns of review cards drift slowly in opposite directions behind soft fade edges. Each card leads with a bold pull-quote. This is the "wall of love" seen on premium software and design studio sites.

Best for
Showing volume and momentum, ideally with 8+ reviews
Strength
Lively and eye-catching. Signals lots of happy clients
Trade-off
With only 4 reviews, the repetition is noticeable

C

Split Showcase

An installation photo with a rating badge sits beside a stacked deck of review cards. Each "next" deals the top card away to reveal the one beneath. It's tactile and ties the praise to real craftsmanship.

Best for
Pairing social proof with your strongest imagery
Strength
Interactive and memorable. Strong on both desktop and mobile
Trade-off
Needs one great photo. Reviews are read one by one

Pull-Quote Ribbon

Strength

Recommendation

With four strong, fairly short reviews, A · Editorial Spotlight gives each one the most weight and matches the editorial tone of the rest of the About page. C · Split Showcase is a close second if you'd like a photo in the section. Revisit B · Wall of Praise once you have eight or more reviews.
