Case Study — Agri-Tourism & Hospitality

Crimson Cider.

Starlink, Wi-Fi and three zones of audio for a Prince Edward County cidery, designed around how guests actually move through the property and finished in time for the May long weekend.

Client
Crimson Cider Co.
Location
Prince Edward County, ON
Scope
Network & Wi-Fi, Zoned Audio, PoE Mic, Control4
Delivered
Victoria Day Weekend 2026
Overview

Crimson Cider wanted the tasting room, the patio and the yard to feel like one place. We designed the network, Wi-Fi and audio as a single system, so guests get good sound wherever they sit and staff never have to think about it.

01 · The Challenge

One speaker.
One big property.

Before the project, music for the whole venue came from a single portable speaker sitting on a table. That's fine for a small room, but not for a timber-framed tasting room, a stone patio and an open yard. Sound was loud in one spot and missing in the next. On top of that, the patio was being dug up and rebuilt that same spring.

What They Needed

Even coverage

Sound spread across every space the way good lighting is, rather than one loud corner.

A dependable connection

A strong internet and Wi-Fi backbone for a rural property, built to carry everything else.

Built with the landscaping

Cabling had to go in alongside the new stonework, not be retrofitted after it.

A hard deadline

Everything running for Victoria Day weekend, the start of the busy season.

Simple for staff

No running between rooms turning volume knobs in the middle of a shift.

02 · The Solution

One system,
planned from the ground up.

01

Designed from the guest's side of the bar

Justin had visited Crimson Cider many times with family and friends long before the project. He already knew how guests drift between the tasting room, the patio and the yard, so the layout was mostly mapped out before the first site visit.

02

Infrastructure under the stone

We worked in step with the excavation crew. Once their base was down, we laid conduit from the building out to both ends of the patio before the gravel and stone went on. The cables are protected from the weight above, and if one is ever damaged it can be pulled and replaced without lifting the patio.

03

A strong backbone first

The part guests never see matters most. A Starlink connection feeds a wired network and ceiling-mounted Wi-Fi, all brought back to one equipment rack. Speed tests on site came in above 300 Mbps download.

04

Three zones, tuned by ear

The tasting room, the exterior patio and the yard each run as their own zone. Inside, we used the same weather-rated speakers as outside, which suit the timber room and keep the sound consistent as guests walk through. Every zone has a tuned EQ profile, a preset volume, and an output ceiling that protects the speakers if someone turns them all the way up.

05

Easy to run, day to day

Everything runs through Control4 on a tablet at the bar. Music streams from the owner's own Apple Music account, so new additions to their playlists show up automatically. The TV is hardwired with its sound routed into the system, and a PoE microphone feeds the same audio matrix for announcements.

3 Independent audio zones

03 · The Result

Music on the way in, the Blue Jays game in the tasting room, a cider at the bar, then back out to the patio, all without a gap in the sound.

That was the walk-through Justin gave the finished system on a visit with his wife earlier this summer. Each space has its own sound, and moving between them feels seamless. For Crimson Cider, that means a venue guests want to linger in, and a system the staff can run with one tap.
